## PLANO 2 — Algo muda no céu

## PLANO 3 — A bola de fogo
**Imagem:**
> Photorealistic cinematic low-angle view from the river looking up at the sky over the Amazon jungle canopy at dawn: a gigantic blazing fireball with a long burning trail of fire and smoke tearing across a sky that is turning blood-red, glowing fragments breaking off. **Animação:**
> The giant fireball streaks diagonally across the sky from upper right to lower left, growing brighter, its fiery trail expanding with smoke, sparks and smaller fragments splitting off. The sky darkens to deep red. Camera tilts slowly following the fireball. 5 seconds.

## PLANO 4 — A explosão
**Imagem:**
> Photorealistic cinematic wide shot from behind the fisherman in his canoe on the wide Amazon river: on the horizon beyond the jungle a massive blinding orange-white explosion lights the whole sky, a rising fireball cloud, the river reflecting the fire, the fisherman's silhouette small in the foreground raising an arm to cover his eyes. 16:9, epic, no text.

**Animação:**
> A blinding flash on the horizon, then a huge fireball cloud rises behind the jungle; a visible shockwave races across the forest bending the treetops toward the camera, flocks of birds explode out of the trees, the river surface ripples and the canoe rocks violently. The fisherman covers his face. Slight camera shake. 5 seconds.

## PLANO 5 — O depois
**Imagem:**
> Photorealistic cinematic wide shot of the Amazon river under a blood-red sky after an explosion: fine red dust and ash slowly falling over the water and the jungle, smoke columns on the horizon, the lone fisherman small in his canoe, standing still, looking at the horizon. Eerie, silent atmosphere. 16:9, no text.

**Animação:**
> Fine red dust and ash keep falling slowly over the river, smoke drifts on the horizon, two more distant flashes light up behind the forest one after the other, the canoe drifts slowly. Very slow camera pull-back. 5 seconds.
